What happens

  • Each bill becomes the record for the month on its statement date, with the PDF attached.
  • A bill for an account the tracker doesn't know yet creates the account and puts it at the property with the same street address.
  • Bills list the payments received since the last one, so importing a new bill marks the earlier one paid.
  • A bill that's already recorded is skipped, so uploading the same file twice is safe.

Supported today: Hydro One, Enbridge Gas, and Municipality of North Grenville water bills. Other bills can be added by hand from Add payment.
