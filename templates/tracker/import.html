What happens

  • Each bill becomes the record for the month on its statement date, with the PDF attached.
  • A bill for an account the tracker doesn't know yet creates the account at the property with the same street address.
  • Payments listed on a new bill mark the earlier bills paid, each on its own date. A bill paid by automatic withdrawal shows as scheduled until the withdrawal date.
  • A bill that's already recorded is skipped, so uploading the same file twice is safe.

Supported bills

Read exactly: Hydro One, Toronto Hydro, Enbridge Gas, Wyse Meter Solutions and North Grenville water.

Read generally: other major Ontario providers, such as Alectra, Hydro Ottawa, Elexicon, London Hydro, Enova Power, city water bills, Bell, Rogers and Telus. The general reader finds the amount due, the dates and the account number, and flags each bill with Check until you open and save it.

Any other company: its name is read from the bill and added to your providers, and its bills are flagged Check. If the name can't be found, choose the provider under “Read unrecognized bills as”.

Scanned bills without text can't be read; add those from Add payment.
